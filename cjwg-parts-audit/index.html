    Counting rules (read first — this is where past counts inflated)¶

    1. Contact sheets / verify sheets are NOT parts. Every category folder contains a _CONTACT_SHEET.png (and eyes an _VERIFY_base-white.png). Eye/mouth counts below exclude them.
    2. Three different kinds of file are mixed in these folders, and only the first is a buildable part:
    3. (A) REAL extracted cutouts — RGBA, real alpha, tight part-bbox canvas, from the character's own pixels: eyes/, arms/, head-hood/, hair-accessories/, hands/, mouths/set-one/, plus the full-common-canvas layers in rig-proof/parts/ + rig-proof/rig-layers/ (RGBA, alpha present — sampled verified).
    4. (B) PACK-GENERATED art — NOT extracted, NOT the characters' own art: hands-gloves/ (20/char), robe/ (ROBE_FRONT/BACK + SLEEVE_L/R, 4/char), feet/ (SHOE_L/R/PAIR, 3/char) in every standard folder. Per the library's own INDEX, these are parametric PIL constructions built in-house 2026-10-29 (glove_gen.py/robe_gen.py). The owner verdict recorded in that INDEX: the gloves "look homemade." They exist and are usable as placeholders, but do not count them as real parts. Battle folders do not contain them at all.
    5. (C) FULL-FRAME, NO ALPHA — NOT parts at all: v1-concept/ (200 PNGs across 8 chars, all verified mode=RGB, no alpha, 1600×1600 full frames with the whole character behind the "part") and ashes/ashes-approved-pack/ (26 PNGs, all RGB, no alpha, also an earlier design iteration — Celtic-knot hood, no braids/grill). These cannot be dropped into a rig as separated parts. They are reference frames only.
    6. rig-proof/parts/00_base_full_figure.png in every folder is a whole-character image (RGBA silhouette on transparent background, e.g. Ashes 400×864, 54% opaque) — not a body part.
    7. No character is "only full-frame whole-character images." Every one of the 9 has real separated cutouts (kind A). But no character is fully buildable to the requested bend spec either — see Universal Gaps. The honest verdict for all 9 is PARTIAL, at different depths.

    Bottom line for the FLA build lane¶

    • Buildable today (limited rig): all 9 as a front, hood-up, robe puppet with head tilt (small), shoulder swing (small/moderate), eye swaps (full, except std Kiko = sparkle/dim only and battle Onyx = open only), mouth swaps (Ashes real; other 8 built/rare-use), and waist motion only for Ashes (cut-apart torso/skirt split). Ashes is the only one with documented pivots — every other character's pivots still have to be set by the builder from the part bboxes.
    • Not buildable from anything on disk, for anyone: elbow bends, hip bends, knee bends, legs, own-art feet, wide neck turns without a gap, arm poses beyond REST, hood-down heads, back/TQ views. Those need a drawn-new art pass — they were not fabricated into these folders, and the pack gloves/robes/shoes that paper over some of them are generator-built placeholders the owner has already called homemade (gloves).
    • Do not count as parts: all v1-concept/ (200 RGB full-frames), all ashes-approved-pack/ (26 RGB full-frames, earlier design), all 00_base_full_figure.png whole-character images, all _CONTACT_SHEET.png / _VERIFY_*.png, and anything under _industry/, _candidates/, character-parts-full-diagrams/, or correct-comparison-to-industry-standard/ (other shows' parts / comparison sheets).
