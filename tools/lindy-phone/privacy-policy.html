Privacy Policy — Orion Enterprises LLC

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Who we are

Orion Enterprises LLC ("we", "us") operates a personal AI-agent messaging system. The system's sole user and sole message recipient is the owner of Orion Enterprises LLC, who provisioned the system for his own agents to reach him.

Messaging program

By opting in (provisioning the system and verifying his own mobile number), the owner consents to receive recurring conversational text messages from his own AI agents (e.g., schedule check-ins, project updates, pattern reviews) at the mobile number he provided. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out; reply HELP for help.

Mobile information — never shared

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile opt-in data and consent are never sold, rented, or shared. Information is used solely to operate the owner's own messaging system.

Data we hold

The owner's mobile number, message contents exchanged with his own agents, and system logs. This data is stored in the owner's private infrastructure and is not disclosed to any third party except the messaging provider strictly as needed to deliver the messages.
