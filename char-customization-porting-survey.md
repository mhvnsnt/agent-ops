## Concrete Dragon world brief (Track 2 — universe-tailored content)

**The world.** Concrete Dragon is an original mobile street brawler, deliberately sealed off from AshLane canon ("NOTHING from AshLane" is a design law in GAME_DESIGN.md). Art direction: gritty hand-drawn 2D with heavy ink outlines and limited palettes per district — graffiti mural meets fight poster. Districts have identities: neon downtown, rust-belt industrial, boardwalk, subway tunnels. Audio: boom-bap / industrial / latin trap per district. Live-service F2P: seasons (8-week "THE TAKEOVER" pass), 3 currencies (Cash soft, Gold hard, Parts), skins are cosmetic-only, 4 rarity tiers (Street/Rare/Epic/Legendary), deterministic rewards (no loot boxes). Monetization runs on LOOKS — customization is a revenue feature here, not just flavor.

**The roster (8, all original).** Rook (balanced boxer, starter), Vex (fast kickboxer), Brick (grappler, slow, huge damage), Mack (weapon specialist), Sable (counter-fighter), Juno (rushdown), Iris (acrobatic, aerial), Hollow Point (boss-tier zoner unlock). No AshLane names, likenesses, or assets — zero.

**The customization stack already in place.**
- Skin system: paper-doll 256×256 painted part textures (`assets/art/<skin>/`), per-fighter tint multiply over the map (green torso/purple arm shifted toward a signature color — never flat), persisted via SaveData, SKIN picker on the select screen. Two DLC packs already: Noir Rook (black/gold, crimson gloves), Crimson Vex (crimson/black, crimson wraps).
- Part-attach spec: `docs/PART_ATTACH_SPEC.md` — bone-attach GLB parts onto the Rigify-style fighter skeleton (head/spine/chest/arms/hands/hips/feet/root slots), all CC0 packs inventoried (Kaykit adventurers/skeletons/halloween, Kenney street/retro/survivor/cube-pets, Quaternius monsters/animals/humanoids).
- LOOK LAW (binding): every part must carry a painted multi-piece texture in the same style; flat untextured parts banned. Per-fighter tint multiply keeps working on attached parts.
- Design already plans 4 gear slots (gloves, jacket, kicks, accessory) × 4 rarities — the suite should FILL these, not invent parallel slots.

**Tailored content direction for the 7 suite items (CD world, never AshLane canon):**
1. **Chain pendant orientation fix** — TECH ports as-is (bone-attach orientation fix on `chest`/`spine` slot); content = district-flavored pendants (dog tags, chunky curb chains, saint medallions) per PART_ATTACH_SPEC texture rules.
2. **Masks** — CD street flavor: ski masks, bandanas, hockey masks, luchador-adjacent (careful: Sombra Negra is AshLane — use generic lucha patterns, not his identity), animal-head masks from kenney-cube-pets/quaternius wolf (beast fighter flavor fits Mack/Brick).
3. **Gloves / wrist pads / shoes** — map 1:1 onto the existing gear slots (gloves→gloves, wrist pads→accessory, shoes→kicks). District palettes: neon-downtown synthetics, industrial work-boot leather, boardwalk canvas. Crimson Vex already shows wraps as the pattern.
4. **Hoods** — attach to `head`/`chest`; street silhouettes: up-hoodies, puffer hoods, sleeveless hooded vests (rust-belt winter flavor).
5. **Face paint system** — CD-native: fight-night paint (cornerman stripes, war paint), graffiti-tag cheek marks, Day-of-the-Dead sugar-skull (fits latin-trap district + spooky Kaykit halloween pack). Painted into skin part textures via `assets/skins/` pipeline.
6. **Hairstyles** — paper-doll head-part variants: fades, braids/locs, mohawks, shaved designs. Must follow the painted-texture law (no flat geometry hair).
7. **Eye color** — simplest: tint variants on the head part texture (head texture already carries eyes in paper-doll style); rarity-gated (e.g., Legendary "Hollow Point" glow eyes).
- **Monetization fit:** bundle suite content into the existing rarity/season machine — Legendary district-exclusive masks, season-pass hoods, shop-rotated glove sets. Every cosmetic must respect the "skins are cosmetic-only, zero stats" law.
- **Hard no:** no AshLane character likenesses, no WWE-named gear, no shared-universe canon references. If a design smells like AshLane, it doesn't ship.

## Wave-ops lesson (2026-10-09): git worktree isolation for shared repos
Two checkout collisions happened when parallel waves shared one working tree (urban-mayhem-port: suite commit 201fd64 landed on the wrong branch; M-Hero-Simulator-: suite worker + genre-research wave on the same checkout). Rule going forward for shared repos (theyard, urban-mayhem-port, M-Hero-Simulator-, any repo with 2+ active waves): each wave works in its OWN `git worktree` (e.g. `<repo>-suite`, `<repo>-research`), never `git checkout` in the shared tree. The coordinator creates the worktree; the worker owns its branches inside it.
