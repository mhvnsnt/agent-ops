    Owner question (verbatim): "what else hasn't been pushed or merged or completed"

    • 69 open PRs audited across the 5 repos. Zero are conflicting except 2 in Bannon. The dominant failure class is not conflict — it is MERGEABLE work sitting unmerged:

    Status classes used below: - STRANDED-READY — payload exists nowhere on main; PR/branch is the only copy; land it. - STRANDED-CI — MERGEABLE but checks not green (quota outage); stranded on CI, not on content. - BLOCKED-PROTECTION — MERGEABLE but branch protection blocks merge (ConcreteDragon). - OWNER-HELD — deliberately unmerged per standing owner decision/exclusion. - DUPLICATE — same payload as a twin PR/branch; land one, close the other. - ALREADY-ON-MAIN — branch contained in main (ahead_by=0); hygiene only. - ORPHAN-HISTORY — no common ancestor with main; cannot PR/merge normally. - NEVER-STARTED / DOES-NOT-EXIST — claimed or owed work that exists nowhere.
